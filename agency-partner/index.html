How it works

You hand it over.
I hand it back ready.

Everything runs through you. I never contact your client, and nothing goes out with my name on it.

  1. 01

    Scope handoff

    You send the brief, the access and the deadline. We agree scope and price on one short call so nothing changes halfway through.

  2. 02

    Execution

    I build inside your ad accounts or on a staging link you control, and post progress in whatever channel your team already uses.

  3. 03

    QA and delivery

    Before it reaches you, I check the tracking, budgets, links and forms. You get it back in a state you can present straight away.

  4. 04

    You deliver it

    It goes to the client as your agency's work. If they want changes, you pass them to me and I turn them around within the agreed window.

How I operate

Quiet, careful,
and on time.

The rules I hold myself to on every partner project, whether it's a single landing page or a full ad account.

I'm happy to sign your NDA before you share a single client detail. Client names, results and assets stay between us, and partner work never appears in my own portfolio unless you say it can.

Deadlines I plan backwards from

When I give you a date, the work is scheduled around it. If anything puts that date at risk, you hear about it early, along with the reason and a new plan.

Checked before it ships
